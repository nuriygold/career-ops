01 · Platform at a glance

One operating model for many systems

The platform connects compute, network, data, security, automation, AI, observability, and recovery into a deliberately owned system. “Private cloud” here means personally owned compute, storage, networking, automation, and service access operated as an integrated platform, not a claim of hyperscale public-cloud capacity.

02 · Architecture

What was engineered

Reliable compute and runtime

Apple Silicon control host, Linux infrastructure, Docker/Compose, databases, CI runners, and persistent services.

Private, segmented access

Starlink edge, Pi-hole DNS/DHCP, Tailscale private access, and Cloudflare Tunnel public ingress.

Guarded AI control plane

Observe state, detect anomalies, recommend actions, execute registered operations, and record audit evidence.

Recoverable operations with proof

Runbooks, rollback gates, backup and restore requirements, health states, soak tests, and evidence-driven verification.

03 · Controlled autonomy

Architecture in motion

Mac mini and Linux runtime
↓
AI control plane and policy checks
↓
OpenClaw watchdog and registered actions
↓
Services, APIs, MCP integrations, and CI
↓
Logs, health states, and recovery evidence
Responsible automation boundary: This is not unsupervised general autonomy. The system operates within registered actions, explicit thresholds, protected configuration boundaries, cooldowns, and human escalation rules.
04 · Recovery reliability

Bounded autonomous recovery

Health check
↓
Failure threshold
↓
Staged restart
↓
Lock + cooldown
↓
Audit result
Example: service failure detected → restart attempted → health rechecked → escalation suppressed after recovery.

A persistent OpenClaw watchdog runs locally every five minutes, checks local and public health, requires sustained failure before acting, follows a staged restart ladder, uses durable state and locking, enforces cooldowns, and preserves surrounding network, security, and application configuration.

05 · Evidence and controls

Proof that the systems are operated

Local runtime

Apple Silicon control host and Debian/Linux infrastructure support persistent services and local AI.

Private access

Tailscale and Cloudflare boundaries separate LAN access, tailnet administration, and public ingress.

Recovery

Watchdog, locks, cooldowns, restart ladder, health thresholds, and sanitized operational logs.

Delivery

APIs, MCP integrations, self-hosted CI, dashboards, audit artifacts, and repeatable verification.

06 · Platform outcome

From components to capability

Consumer hardware operates as a maintainable private cloud and operations center. Local AI connects to real workflows, APIs and MCP integrations reach infrastructure capabilities, self-hosted CI supports delivery, and security controls limit the blast radius of automation.

07 · Operating principle

Complexity is managed, not denied

Complexity is managed through ownership, evidence, documentation, automation, and controlled recovery. The goal is not to eliminate complexity, but to make it observable, explainable, and recoverable.
